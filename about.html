Our Culinary Story

Our Story

Aryan Khan Restaurant is a modern family dining destination where traditional flavors meet contemporary dining. Our goal is to provide delicious food, quality ingredients and memorable hospitality for every guest.

Founded over a decade ago in Islamabad, AKR started with a passion for bringing authentic charcoal grilled BBQ and slow-cooked Pakistani handis to local food enthusiasts. Today, we continue that tradition with master chefs and modern dining standards.
